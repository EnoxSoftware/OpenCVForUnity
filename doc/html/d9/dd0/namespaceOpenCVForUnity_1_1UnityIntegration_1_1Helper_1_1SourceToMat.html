enum OpenCVForUnity.UnityIntegration.Helper.SourceToMat.MultiSourceHelperKind : int

Built-in SourceToMatHelperBase kinds that MultiSourceToMatHelper can attach as the single active helper on its GameObject.

WebCam Sync and AsyncGPU readback share MultiSourceHelperKind.WebCamTexture; AsyncGPU preference is stored in the WebCam per-kind cache (WebCamTextureSourceInspectorSettings.RequestedUseAsyncGPUReadback) rather than as a separate enum value.
