◆ StopAndDestroyWebCamTexture()

static void OpenCVForUnity.UnityIntegration.Helper.SourceToMat.Grabbers.WebCamTextureGrabberUtils.StopAndDestroyWebCamTexture ( WebCamTexture webCamTexture)
static

Stops and destroys a WebCamTexture so the exclusive camera device is released.

Always calls WebCamTexture.Stop before UnityEngine.Object.DestroyImmediate(UnityEngine.Object). During scene unload, WebCamTexture.isPlaying can be false while the OS device is still held; gating Stop on isPlaying leaves the camera locked across LoadScene. Pause also leaves isPlaying false without releasing the device. Immediate destroy is required so Heavy re-init and PlayMode tests can reopen without waiting for end-of-frame UnityEngine.Object.Destroy(UnityEngine.Object).

Parameters
webCamTextureThe texture to stop and destroy; ignored when null.
