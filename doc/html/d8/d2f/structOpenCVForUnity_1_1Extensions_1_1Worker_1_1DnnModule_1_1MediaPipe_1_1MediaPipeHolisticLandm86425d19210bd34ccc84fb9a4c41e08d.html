Detailed Description

One-frame inference result from HolisticLandmarkerGraph before PackResultsToMats. This is analogous to MediaPipePoseLandmarkerBase<OpenCVForUnity.Extensions.Worker.DnnModule.IDnnInferenceNet>'s List<PoseResult>.

Pose's HolisticPoseTrackFrameResult.SegmentationMaskFull remains intermediate-owned until it is passed to BuildHolisticPackedOutputs; after packing it is released or reused according to the worker's internal policy. See HolisticPoseTrackFrameResult remarks for details.
