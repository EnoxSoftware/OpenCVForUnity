◆ LayoutAffectingSettingsChanged

Action OpenCVForUnity.Extensions.SourceToMat.DerivedFrame.DerivedFrameCollection.LayoutAffectingSettingsChanged

Occurs when a derived frame is newly registered or when layout-affecting settings (DerivedFrameSettings.ScaleRatio / DerivedFrameSettings.OutputColorFormat) change.

Hosts such as MatSourceBase use this to re-sync output buffers immediately so LayoutChanged can fire without waiting for the next source-frame tick. Non-layout updates such as DerivedFrameSettings.ProcessEveryNFrames do not raise this event.
