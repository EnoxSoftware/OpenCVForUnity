Detailed Description

Optional playback control for Unity SourceToMat grabbers that drive a playing capture device or player.

Implement on grabbers such as UnityVideoPlayerFrameGrabber and WebCam texture grabbers. Still-image or static-texture grabbers omit this interface. Prefer SourceToMatHostMarshaling.RunOnCapturedMainThreadAsync for these methods.
